A parent's guide

MathsStudio follows the Scottish Curriculum for Excellence from First level onwards. Every year group is split into topics, and every topic works the same way. Here is how to get the most from it.

Get started

1

Sign up with a parent or teacher email

It is free to start, and no card is needed. Accounts belong to the adult, and we never collect a child's email.

2

Add a profile for each child

Once you are signed in, add your child with a first name or a nickname, plus an avatar. Each child keeps their own progress and badges. No photos are used.

3

Pick the year group

P2 to S3 are ready now. Start at Topic 1 and work through the topics in order.

In every topic

1

Let's Learn

Start with the short animated lesson, which is read aloud. Then step-by-step lessons with worked examples. Watch the first one together.

2

Activity Time

Hands-on activities and puzzles where your child can build, slide and explore. This is where ideas stick.

3

Let's Practise

A warm-up, then hundreds of questions at Low, Medium and High, with hints and a scribble pad. Move up a level when the current one feels easy. Each topic ends with real-life problems that put the maths to use.

Checking progress

10-minute mental maths test (P4 onwards). Try it after your child has finished all of the year's topics. It has questions from every topic, so it shows whether the learning has stuck.
Check my strengths (P4 onwards). Use it at any time to see which topics are strong and which need more work. Both are on your child's home page.
Progress report. Scores for each check and a colour-coded topic map, ready to save as a PDF or print.

Need more help?

If your child is stuck on something, use Request a lesson from your signed-in page and tell us what you need. We add new lessons, activities and questions on a regular basis.

Free to try

All of P2 and P3 is free, and Topic 1 of every other year group is free too, with all its lessons, activities and practice. No card is needed.
