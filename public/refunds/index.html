Refunds and cancelling

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Cancelling a monthly plan

Sign in, open Account and press Manage subscription. You can cancel in a minute. You keep access until the end of the month you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again. We do not give part-month refunds after the month has started.

Our 14-day money-back promise

If MathsStudio is not right for you, email info@mathsstudio.co.uk within 14 days of your first payment for a year group and we will refund it in full: the first £2.99 month, or the £15 year. We usually reply within two working days, and the refund goes back to the card or account you paid with.

Your legal right to cancel

Under UK consumer law you can normally cancel an online purchase within 14 days. Because MathsStudio is digital and your access starts straight away, we ask you to agree before paying that access begins immediately and that you then lose the legal right to cancel once you start using it. Our 14-day promise above is something extra we offer anyway, so you are not left out of pocket.

Yearly passes

A yearly pass does not renew. It simply ends after 12 months and you decide whether to buy another. After the first 14 days we do not refund the rest of a yearly pass, unless we withdraw the year group or the site cannot be used for a long period.

If something goes wrong

  • Paid but not unlocked? Sign out and back in. If it is still locked, email us and we will fix it, or refund you.
  • Charged twice or by mistake? Email us with the email address on your account and we will put it right.
  • Not happy with the maths? Tell us. We want to know, and we will fix it.
