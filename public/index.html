Every topic has three parts

1

Let's Learn

Short lessons teach each idea three ways: how it works, where it turns up in real life, and a deeper look. Then worked examples to try, from getting started to challenge.

2

Activity Time

Hands-on labs and games to explore the idea: builders, sliders, Beat the clock, Race the robot, Match-up and Question detective.

3

Let's Practice

A quick warm-up, then 100 questions at Low, Medium or High with hints and a scribble pad. Finish with real-life problems about shopping, travel and sport.

Read aloud

Turn the voice on and each new question is read out.

Scribble pad

A pen pad under each question to work out sums on screen.

Every strand covered

Number, fractions, measure, shape, data, algebra, ratio and spatial reasoning, for each year from P5 to S3.

Clear feedback

Hints, worked explanations and a clear Brilliant or Missed it.
