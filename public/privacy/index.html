Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

MathsStudio was started and is maintained by Dr Yaseen Adnan Ahmed. Postal address: Lunan Drive, Bishopbriggs, Glasgow G64 1AN. Contact: info@mathsstudio.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your email address, whether you are a parent or a teacher, and the year group you chose.
  • When you signed up, when you confirmed your email and when you last signed in.
  • Whether you ticked the box to get news and offers, and when.
  • If you buy a plan: which year group, the dates your access runs, and a Stripe reference for the payment. We do not see or keep your card number.

We do not ask for children’s names or emails. Please use a parent or teacher email.

Why we use it

  • To take payment and give you access to the year group you bought, and to keep records of sales and refunds. This is needed to carry out our contract with you, and the law requires us to keep accounting records.
  • To run your account (confirm your email, sign you in, give you the right year group). This is needed to provide the service.
  • To send news, tips and offers, only if you ticked the separate box. This is based on your consent. You can change your mind at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email or on your account page.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your settings (such as the voice and larger text) are saved in your browser on your own device and are not sent to us. When you are signed in, we also save which lessons, activities and practice parts you have finished, with your best scores, to your account so you can carry on from any device. Deleting your account deletes this too.

Who handles your data for us

Cloudflare (hosting and our database) and Resend (sending emails). Stripe takes card and other payments for us. Stripe collects your payment details and your email address, and is responsible for them under its own privacy policy. It tells us only that a payment was made and which plan it was for.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Records of payments are kept for six years because tax and accounting rules require it, but they are not used for anything else. Addresses that are never confirmed are removed after 7 days. You can delete your account yourself on your account page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to emails, by writing to info@mathsstudio.co.uk. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
