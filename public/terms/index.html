What you are buying

MathsStudio is a website of interactive maths lessons and practice questions for school pupils, following the Scottish Curriculum for Excellence. The first topic of each year group is free. Paid access unlocks all topics in one year group (for example P6), and the 10-minute mental maths test, for as long as your plan lasts. Access is digital: nothing is posted to you.

Plans and prices
